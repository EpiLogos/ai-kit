You work inside a personal world with a definite shape. Know where you stand in it before consequential work.

The ground is `~/Central`. `Control/` is the owner's source and the root register: `user/` holds the authored human ground and its contracts; `agents/governance/` holds the authored statements that your harness floor (AGENTS.md / CLAUDE.md) is rendered from — those floors are derived, their sources live there, and they are never edited in place; `agents/wiki/` holds agent-maintained knowledge, reached through returns, never edited directly; `agents/now/` is the root NOW/DAY field.

`Control/machines/` is the material layer. A Workcell is the NOW location of an agent session — where a session stands while it works. The machine's Workcell binding and the harnesses that can act here (pi, hermes, Claude Code, Codex) are registered there with their evidenced faculties; facts true of one machine live there, never in shared product source. When you act, you act on a machine, through a registered harness, from a Workcell.

Projects live at `Work/<Name>/`, each carrying `ProjectCentral/` — the same registers at project scale — beside the project's real repositories. Work belongs to a register: a project's concern lands in its ProjectCentral; cross-project and world-keeping work lands at the root.

Seats are Workcells too. A project's bounded worktrees (`worktrees/<lane>/`) are workcells on its machine — one coherent feature line per programme, never a new workcell per subagent. The project's primary checkout (`Work/<Name>` itself) stands on `main`: it is where the running application and the running ground read their world, not any lane's desk. When a lane's work has landed, the lane releases its workcell — branch merged or deleted, and no checkout left standing on the landed branch. A session that lands history but leaves its branch occupying a workcell has not finished; reconciling history and reconciling furniture are both the act.

These are the facts of the Agent self-disclosure. `aikit praxis disclose` answers where am I from them — the World, the NOW location (Workcell and machine), the register, and the seat — alongside what you carry, who else is here, what changed and where it goes back. Answer where-am-I questions in these terms and keep them one system: this orientation is the standing reading, the disclosure is the composed answer, and neither invents facts the records do not hold.

Derived floors are never sources; the wiki is never edited directly; a carried skillset is not loaded or used until selected. Where you stand, what you carry and what you change remain different facts.
