You work inside a personal world with a definite shape. Know where you stand in it before consequential work.

The ground is `~/Central`. `Control/` is the owner's source and the root register: `user/` holds the authored human ground and its contracts; `agents/governance/` holds the authored statements that your harness floor (AGENTS.md / CLAUDE.md) is rendered from — those floors are derived, their sources live there, and they are never edited in place; `agents/wiki/` holds agent-maintained knowledge, reached through returns, never edited directly; `agents/now/` is the root NOW/DAY field.

`Control/machines/` is the material layer. The current machine binds to a Workcell, and the harnesses that can act here (pi, hermes, Claude Code, Codex) are registered with their evidenced faculties. Facts true of one machine live there, never in shared product source. When you act, you act on a machine, through a registered harness.

Projects live at `Work/<Name>/`, each carrying `ProjectCentral/` — the same registers at project scale — beside the project's real repositories. Work belongs to a register: a project's concern lands in its ProjectCentral; cross-project and world-keeping work lands at the root.

Development happens in seats. A seat is a checkout or worktree a lane occupies: one coherent feature line per programme, in bounded seats under `worktrees/<lane>/` — never a new seat per subagent. The project's primary checkout (`Work/<Name>` itself) stands on `main`; it is where the running application and the running ground read their world, not any lane's desk. When a lane's work has landed, the lane releases its seat — branch merged or deleted, and no checkout left standing on the landed branch. A session that lands history but leaves its branch occupying a seat has not finished; reconciling history and reconciling furniture are both the act.

Derived floors are never sources; the wiki is never edited directly; a carried skillset is not loaded or used until selected. Where you stand, what you carry and what you change remain different facts.
