# Verification and conformance

Semantic ref: `aikit:verification`. Native owner: `EpiLogos/ai-kit`.

## Whole-relative verification law

Verification begins from the **Claim and the operative Whole it purports to determine**, not from whichever test or artifact is easiest to produce. An Agent-authored Claim is a proposed determination: plausibility, coherence and confidence do not enlarge the scope of the evidence beneath it.

For any consequential statement equivalent to `done`, `complete`, `adequate`, `satisfied`, `fixed`, `verified` or `conformant`, establish the verification field first:

```text
Claim
  -> operative Whole / exact subject state
  -> materially relevant obligations of that Whole
  -> owner-native proving operation for each required obligation
  -> exact Evidence / Assessment / Review standing
  -> unresolved / contradicted / blocked / inapplicable obligations remain visible
  -> Closure only at the scope actually warranted
```

The Whole may be disclosed by an owner-native acceptance contract, an authored Intent/requirement set, a Project capability matrix, a QL-shaped relational field, a RunMap region or another attributable structure. AIKit does not invent a rival verification ontology for those sources; it resolves and executes the appropriate proving paths while preserving their identity and scope.

A green check proves the condition it actually checks. It does not prove neighbouring requirements by plausibility. A screenshot proves only what can actually be established from that screenshot. A successful representative sample proves the whole only when the effective verification requirement declares the sampling/coverage rule sufficient and that coverage condition is itself evidenced. A producer's report that a deterministic command passed is not equivalent to rerunning or inspecting the proving operation when the verifier can do so directly.

Do not silently shrink the operative Whole after execution until the completed subset becomes a plausible definition of success. If one material obligation remains unsupported, report the Claim as partial / insufficiently evidenced / blocked / contradicted according to the native owner semantics. `n-1` satisfied obligations are not full Closure merely because the remaining obligation is inconvenient.

Where materially independent obligations can be checked independently, preserve that independence and verify them concurrently when appropriate. Where independent Assessment or human Review is required, preserve the required independent execution/reviewer lineage rather than allowing the producing Agent's confidence to stand in for scrutiny.

## Procedure

1. Identify the **Claim, exact subject state, operative Whole and materially relevant verification obligations**. Then identify the changed public contract(s) and owner-native proof paths that bear on those obligations. Do not begin from a convenient test list and infer the completion Claim afterwards.
2. Run the narrow conformance/proving operation for each required obligation first, then the repository baseline appropriate to the change. Record which obligation each result actually establishes; keep uncovered obligations explicit.
3. For capsules/Skills, validate manifest path/id, payload root/frontmatter, source/revision and registry scan behaviour. If the Claim is broader than those properties, those checks are partial evidence until the remaining obligations are proved.
4. For UsageOverlays, verify the unchanged Skill source, exact scoped adaptation/review digest and resulting Effective Skill projection; a projected file never becomes source.
5. For Methods, verify source identity/revision, stable independently owned member refs, exact overlay digests where used, expected verification/return forms and resolution under the existing ContextResolution. Method selection must not activate, trust or authorise a referenced resource.
6. For SkillSets, verify explicit membership, additive resolution and withholding of members that fail their own gates. SkillSet union must remain independent from Method composition and Profile precedence.
7. For Project reflection, verify explicit ProjectMap bindings and both semantic→code and code→semantic traversal at bounded depth. Keep authored Ground, SemanticWiki, local structural description, CodeReference, CodeIndex observation, verification/evidence and history differently authoritative.
8. If the target declares a strong reflection law, test missing, wrong, duplicate and stale mappings plus constitutive structural relations. Matching names are not parity; structural flattening must fail even when labels survive.
9. For local structural descriptions, include at least one stale-description/reflection case. The result is discrepancy evidence routed to the source owner, not permission for the verifier to rewrite the description/Wiki/Ground.
10. For projection, verify target-compatible effective material plus provenance/receipt/generation; never bless the projection as authoritative source.
11. For provider/runtime changes, include degraded/absent/unauthorised paths and the real provider activation boundary.
12. Reconcile the evidence against the original Whole and Claim. Record exact source head, provider/version evidence, tests/workflow run, the obligation each piece establishes, and any skipped/external/live owner requirement. Green conformance is evidence for owner review, not self-promotion. Do not emit full completion language while a material verification obligation lacks the standing required by the effective plan.

## Project reflection acceptance

A meaningful Project reflection receipt should be able to demonstrate, where those representations exist:

```text
human-authored Ground / Canon
        ↓
SemanticWiki / Project language
        ↕ explicit ProjectMap binding
native local description / scoped contract
        ↕
exact CodeReference
        ↕
CodeIndex structural intelligence
        ↓
verification / evidence / history
        ↺ returned difference
```

From a semantic anchor, prove the route to exact implementation and evidence. From the exact CodeReference, prove the reverse route to known meaning/description/ownership. Provider-native graph richness is not required to be copied into ProjectMap.

For an ordinary contrasting Project with no Central directories, Bimba/QL coordinates, special local-header convention or Method source tree, verify that completed Knowledge Navigation and ordinary native Skill operation still work. Rich reflection/praxis is developmental capacity, not a new minimum-validity condition.

## Strong target-owned conformance

When a target owns a stable coordinate identity and reflection law, consume that law as source-owned conformance input rather than hard-coding the target ontology into AIKit. An Epi/QL specimen may therefore prove the generic mechanism while AIKit remains ignorant of what an `Mx/Mx′`, Bimba category or other target coordinate means.

If the exact owner source required for a stronger claim is unavailable, stop at the strongest honestly source-backed fixture and record the missing owner-level boundary. Do not fabricate a semantic coordinate in order to make the test look complete.
