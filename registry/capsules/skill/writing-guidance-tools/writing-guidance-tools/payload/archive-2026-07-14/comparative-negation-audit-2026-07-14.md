# Comparative and Negation Audit — 2026-07-14

## Scope

The pass covered the sole live central plan, all live section and argument nodes, authored commentary in the scholarly source bank, and the core-theorem spine. It also promoted the local writing guides into the project's governing workflow through `CLAUDE.md`, the source-bank protocol, `WRITING-RUBRIC.md`, and the new [[Comparative and Negation Gate]].

## Editorial movement

The revisions follow four recurrent operations:

1. **Positive definition:** “AHI is not a super-subject” became a definition of AHI as the reflective activity of a coupled field, followed by the consequence that intelligence belongs to its relations and returns.
2. **Mechanism before verdict:** “the pathology is not division” became a description of a runtime arrested after decomposition and before recomposition.
3. **Scoped comparison:** “this source does not prove QL” became a positive statement of what the source warrants, followed by the additional inference and the evidence it would require.
4. **Level distinction:** mathematical object, psychoid interpretation, experiential archive, and metaphysical inference are now named in their own registers before a bridge is drawn.

## Retained negation classes

Negation remains where it performs one of the following necessary jobs:

- **Formal negation:** apoha determines through exclusion; `1/0` remains undefined in an affine field; the crossed stroke does not fill zero; ground is not mark even while both belong to one appearing-act.
- **Determinate apophasis:** the subject cannot be exhausted as an ordinary object, and the failure of objectification defines the exact condition being marked.
- **Technical or historical fact:** a torus flow of irrational slope does not close periodically; the consulted Wittgenstein translation has not yet been selected; Whitehead's later process metaphysics takes a path distinct from enlarged logical closure.
- **Claim boundary:** functional agency alone does not establish phenomenal subjectivity; correspondence between a tattvic apparatus and a computational stack leaves ontological equivalence open.
- **Verified or protected source language:** quoted and imported authors retain their own syntax, including antithesis.

These are earned uses because deleting the negative would remove a boundary, operation, or state of knowledge. Future audits should classify them rather than mechanically rewrite them.

## Governing outcome

The local `writing-guidance-tools/` directory is now the first-class authority for essay prose. Every drafting pass loads the positive voice references, every revision applies the fault catalogue and comparison gate, and every ship-note accounts for retained comparisons. Source records follow the same prose rule while preserving the source object untouched.
