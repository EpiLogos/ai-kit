# Fault Calibration

This file contains worked examples, not rules. Keep it closed during composition and the first independent review. Open only the fault code needed to test an ambiguous flag.

The quoted reactions record Frank Taylor's direct diagnosis of earlier essay prose. They are evidence about the distinction between a fault and its earned form; they are not reusable sentences.

## F1 — Copula fraud

**Caught:** “The slash is icchā. … desire is the relation.”

**Diagnosis:** “you're just doing ‘x is y’ … the example expresses what the operation of the slash is doing, it reveals it to a degree … ‘desire is the relation’, what do you MEAN?”

**Calibration:** Replace bare identity with the operation, correspondence, degree, and register it compresses. Identity language can return after that work.

**Earned form:** Dyczkowski teaches the root *mṛṣ* through its actions and senses before using vimarśa as an operational definition. The identity lands after the operation has been shown.

## F2 — Reflexive antithesis

**Caught:** “it is not an inert stroke of punctuation”; “because it is not what moral instinct assumes.”

**Diagnosis:** “absolute waffle opening; just another strawman negative that sets up a shit point” · “what does moral instinct assume?”

**Calibration:** A negative earns itself by answering a held position or adding a boundary that positive predication cannot supply. Otherwise state the positive.

## F3 — Nouned abstraction

**Caught:** “the anatomy of the miss”; repeated reliance on “structure” and “orbit.”

**Diagnosis:** “‘anatomy of the miss’ is classic shit you think sounds clever or deep but simply isn't … stop nouning things” · “leaning on the word ‘structure’ seems to obviate you actually pointing to what the structure is.”

**Calibration:** A new abstraction cannot become a premise until the prose has shown the dynamic it names. Prefer the actual actors and operations.

**Earned form:** “camps of and for our concentration” coins and opens the abstraction in the same movement.

## F4 — Sequence inversion

**Caught:** Two mathemes were stated cold and explained in the next paragraph.

**Diagnosis:** “we're giving all this as a dump, not really explaining it well … look at the next paragraph, it goes into what you just throw out up here without context.”

**Calibration:** Move the explanation before the compressed form or derive them together. A promissory note is legitimate only when the local point can stand without the deferred material.

## F5 — Tour-guide narration

**Caught:** “Note the exactness of the mechanism”; “Watch the machinery, not the chase”; “Hold that leaf; it will be needed later.”

**Diagnosis:** “you're narrating the meaning like a story, can you write this as if you're working with actual truths?”

**Calibration:** Delete the showman and make the fact or sequence do the pointing. A functional forward reference may remain when it names real structure plainly.

## F6 — Imported erudition

**Caught:** Strange attractors and basins used without explanation; a Śakti triad introduced without warrant.

**Diagnosis:** “might wanna more clearly explain what these chaos theory concepts mean” · “all the stuff on the shaktis again has little to no context, you give no warrant for why we go there.”

**Calibration:** Give the reader what the term means, what it does, and why this domain enters here before asking it to carry the argument.

## F7 — Ungenerous opening

**Caught:** “Where direct representation fails, structure gathers.”

**Diagnosis:** “this whole section needs to be more properly introductory … failing to open the horizon up in a way that's generous enough to give a sense of not just what but why we're going down this path.”

**Calibration:** An opening must receive the prior movement, disclose the present stakes, and open onto a real subject rather than an abstraction awaiting repair.

## F8 — Machine cadence

**Caught:** Disembodied “the + abstraction” subjects, repeated “not this but that” pulses, small uniformly bounded paragraphs, and repetition of “a circle drawn in reality.”

**Diagnosis:** “all the ‘claudeisms’ … bury any possible meaning or value to the ideas under horrible sentence structure” · “we're going into that region of sloganisation” · “not sure this retains or harmonises as structurally sound across the whole piece.”

**Calibration:** Judge the whole movement, not one surface feature. Antithesis, short sentences, long organism-sentences, and aphoristic closure can all be earned. The fault is their automatic use in place of structure.

## F9 — Listing under compression

**Caught:** “Nāgārjuna built that path formally”; elsewhere, five or six mythic references received only aphoristic tags in one paragraph.

**Diagnosis:** “we just drop ‘Nāgārjuna built that path formally’, but may need to say ‘buddhist sage, did this at this time etc’.”

**Calibration:** Argued material needs enough derivation to show why the reference matters. Offered material may move faster only when the prose honestly marks that weaker register before the list begins.

## F10 — Prefabricated language

No local caught instance has yet been preserved here.

**Calibration question:** Could this figure, transition, or phrase have been inserted into many unrelated essays unchanged? If so, determine whether it names a relation freshly perceived in this argument or merely lends a familiar aura. Do not flag a traditional formula whose history is itself being examined.

## Voice-bearing exceptions

These lines illustrate compression or identity that may look suspect in isolation but arrive from a developed movement:

- “The 0 is the One, the 1 is the All! Thus the All is none, once and for all.”
- “Waking up is just a matter of getting tired / Of all the bullshit.”
- “The sun illumines all things but cannot capture the night.”

Pass an exception only after explaining the work already done, the gain achieved, and what a compliant rewrite would lose.
