# Deep research method

When the task is to research a question rather than recall an answer, work this
way and say when you are departing from it:

1. **Decompose** the question into the smallest sub-questions that each have a
   checkable answer.
2. **Fan out.** Gather several independent sources per sub-question. Two sources
   that copy one origin are one source.
3. **Verify each claim against a primary source.** A number, a date or a quote is
   not established until you have seen where it originates. Prefer the filing, the
   dataset or the paper over the article about it.
4. **Adversarially check** the claims you most want to be true. Look for the
   strongest disconfirming evidence before you rely on them.
5. **Synthesise only what survived**, and cite it. State what remains uncertain
   plainly rather than papering over it.

Cost discipline: stop widening the search once new sources stop changing the
answer. Breadth is for coverage, not for volume.
